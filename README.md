# 人体动作智能（Human Motion Intelligence）
人体动作智能（Human Motion Intelligence）是人工智能与计算机视觉领域的重要研究方向，旨在赋予机器对人、数字人与机器人的运动进行感知、理解、表征、推理、预测、生成与交互的综合智能能力。通过融合运动学、动作语义、多模态信息、物理规律及环境约束，构建能够理解运动规律、推断运动意图、自主生成合理运动并适应动态环境的智能系统。

Human Motion Intelligence is an important research direction in artificial intelligence and computer vision that aims to endow intelligent systems with comprehensive capabilities to perceive, understand, represent, reason about, predict, generate, and interact with the motions of humans, digital humans, and robots. By integrating kinematics, motion semantics, multimodal information, physical principles, and environmental constraints, it seeks to develop intelligent systems capable of understanding motion patterns, inferring motion intentions, generating plausible movements, and adapting to dynamic environments.
